Nesi family pilot agreement

Thanks for helping us test Nesi (NEH-see). The pilot is free and runs Nov 9 to Dec 21, 2026. It's offered by Mike Letterle (individual) ("we"). Please read this page before you join. You'll accept it in the app with one tap (I agree) the first time you open Nesi, before you can use it. A copy is always in the app at Settings › Legal (Terms of Service, Pilot Agreement, Pilot Privacy Policy, Quote my feedback), and online at https://mikeletterle-sys.github.io/pilot-docs/legal/pilot-agreement.html [VERIFY URL]. That page isn't listed in search engines. You must be 18 or older and a parent or guardian in the household. You accept for your household, including any partner or caregiver you invite.

1. It's a free beta. Nesi is unfinished test software. It will have bugs, features may change or disappear, and it may sometimes be unavailable or lose data. Please keep your own copy of anything important. We provide it "as is" [counsel: warranty disclaimer and limitation of liability].

2. Ideas, not advice. Nesi, including the AI assistant, gives general ideas for meals, groceries, chores, activities and your calendar. It is not medical, nutrition, allergy or safety advice, and it can make mistakes.

  • You stay responsible for supervising your kids and for deciding what's safe for your family, including any activity or outing.
  • Always read food labels and check for allergens yourself. The allergy filter is there to help you. It is not a guarantee.
  • Confirm event times, prices and safety details with the organizer.
  • Be careful what you share with the AI. Kids' and adults' names are masked, but other words you type and any photos you add are sent to our AI provider as they are. The app shows a short notice before a photo or a long message goes to the AI. Please leave out health details you'd rather not share, and crop photos as you see fit.

3. Keep it confidential. Please keep the app, the TestFlight link, screenshots and the name "Nesi" within your household until we launch publicly. Don't post about them or share them. It's fine to tell a friend you're testing a new family planner. If someone wants to join, send them to us.

4. Feedback. We welcome all of it, good and bad. You agree that we may use your feedback to improve the app, with no payment or obligation to you. Your feedback stays private unless you turn on the optional quote permission below.

5. Your pilot code. We give your family a pilot code (for example "P07"). You agree that we may link that code to how your family uses the app (for example, whether a weekly plan was accepted) and to your check-in notes and survey answers, so we can write a pilot readout. To make the match, at your first check-in call you'll read us a short code shown in the app. [VERIFY: where the app shows the code.] The readout shows totals and counts. It never includes your name, your kids' names or ages, or any health or allergy details. The Pilot Privacy Policy explains what the app stores.

6. No fees, no payments. The pilot costs nothing and nothing is billed. We don't offer gift cards, cash or other payments for taking part.

7. Founding price. When Nesi launches in the App Store, your family can subscribe at the founding price of $69.99/yr (regular price $89.99/yr). You start with a 14-day free trial. The price stays at $69.99 for as long as you stay subscribed. You can claim it until 30 days after launch. It doesn't depend on how much you use the app or whether you finish the survey, and you keep it even if you leave the pilot early. Subscribing is optional, is billed by Apple, and follows the Subscription Terms. If the app launches under a different name, the promise still applies.

8. Leaving and your data. Either of us can end your participation at any time, for any reason, by telling the other. In the app, a parent can tap Settings › Delete everything to remove the family's data and their own sign-in. Every other adult also taps Settings › Account › Delete account. Or email us and we'll delete your family's data and sign-ins within [30] days. Deleting a sign-in also disconnects Sign in with Apple and Google. If you want to double-check, it's optional: tap Stop Using for Nesi in iPhone Settings › [your name] › Sign-In & Security › Sign in with Apple. Then delete the app. If your family doesn't continue with Nesi after the pilot, we delete your family's data 90 days after the pilot ends (by Mar 21, 2027). Copies our service providers already hold (for example, logs) are removed on their own schedules, as the Pilot Privacy Policy explains. We may also end the pilot early for everyone.

9. The fine print. This agreement and the Pilot Privacy Policy are the whole deal for the pilot.
